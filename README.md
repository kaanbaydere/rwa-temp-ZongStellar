🏢 Stellahouze – Tokenized Room Rental Platform

A Stellar-based platform for tokenizing rooms and letting investors earn revenue from their rentals. Users can tokenize their rooms as assets, sell fractional ownership, and buyers earn a share of rental income.

🎯 Project Overview

Stellahouze democratizes access to room rentals by tokenizing rooms on the Stellar blockchain. Room owners can sell fractional tokens of their rooms, and investors earn rental income proportionally.

🌟 Key Features
For Investors

💼 Dashboard – Real-time portfolio overview of tokenized rooms
🏪 Room Marketplace – Discover and filter tokenized room assets
💸 Income Tracking – Monitor rental income and token performance
🔐 Wallet Integration – Connect Freighter wallet seamlessly

For Room Owners

🏭 Tokenization Wizard – Tokenize a room in 5 steps
📋 Compliance Management – KYC/AML for rental token sales
📈 Revenue Management – Track rental earnings for investors
🔒 Legal Framework – Room documentation and compliance

Platform Features

🌐 Room Asset Marketplace – Multiple room types supported
⚡ Stellar Integration – Fast, low-cost blockchain transactions
🛡️ Compliance – Built-in KYC/whitelist validation
📱 Responsive Design – Works on web, tablet, and mobile
